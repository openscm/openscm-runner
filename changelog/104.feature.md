Added `RunMode.CONCENTRATION_DRIVEN` support to the MAGICC7 adapter for CO2, CH4 and N2O, driving the model from `Atmospheric Concentrations|*` inputs (overlaid on the RCMIP3 baseline) instead of emissions. Mirrors the FaIR2 / CICEROSCMPY2 per-gas mixed-mode behaviour and requires `rcmip3_bundle_path`.
